**Thematic Mapping Framework for Classical Source Material**

**Purpose**  
This framework offers a systematic, universal process for transforming *any* provided source text—whether a raw translation, commentary, interpretation, modern article, user writing, or other material—into refined thematic components. It extracts discrete notions, validates their internal logic, and synthesizes them into coherent, reusable content suitable for websites, apps, infographics, contemplative writings, or other media. The approach emphasizes fidelity to the original text while revealing clear thematic progressions and logical entailments.

**Core Principles**  
- Analyses remain strictly anchored in the provided source text (translation, commentary, interpretation, or any other material).  
- Themes are derived exclusively from the supplied material, with a maximum of 5 per passage.  
- Grouping highlights natural logical flow and mutual entailment between ideas.  
- Tone stays scholarly yet accessible, preserving the nuance and philosophical depth of the source.  
- All outputs integrate seamlessly into a master thematic inventory for ongoing synthesis.

**Project Workflow Guidelines**

1. **Input** — Receive any provided source text (translation, commentary, interpretation, modern writing, user material, etc.).  
2. **Analysis** — Apply the template above, limiting to 3–5 themes and documenting logical entailment.  
3. **Synthesis** — Integrate validated themes into larger narrative sections or Notion Inventory.  
4. **Spruce & Polish** — Refine summary statements for clarity, rhythm, and engagement when requested.  
5. **Iteration** — Maintain version control and revisit earlier sections as new material emerges.  

**Master Thematic Inventory Structure (Recommended for thematic_mapping.md)**

- **Core Essence Statement** (spruced opening portrait of the overall concept)  
- **Image / Foundational Symbolism** (thematic breakdown)  
- **Line-by-Line Analyses** (using the standardized template)  
- **Cross-Cutting Notions** (recurring themes across sections)  
- **Synthesized Applications** (ready-to-use content for websites, apps, or writings)
