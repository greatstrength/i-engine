# Step 3: Thematic Enhancement Pass  
## Procedure & Template for Jian 漸 Thematic Inventory

**Purpose**  
This step follows the standardized Thematic Citation Breakdown (Analysis Stage). It takes the analyzed interpretation(s) of a hexagram line, compares them with any prior mappings or multiple sources (e.g., Wilhelm/Baynes and Karcher), validates themes for strict fidelity, and produces a polished, cohesive paragraph or section ready for the master inventory. The goal is improved clarity, rhythm, logical flow, and usability while never adding ideas outside the provided source material.

**When to Apply**  
- After receiving a new standardized thematic analysis.  
- When refining a user-provided “official snippet.”  
- When harmonizing multiple interpretations of the same line.  
- As a final quality check before integration.

**Core Principles**  
- Strict fidelity to the supplied interpretation texts and line wording.  
- Preserve the natural logical progression and philosophical nuance of *Jian* 漸.  
- Maintain a scholarly yet warm and accessible tone.  
- Focus on enhancements in clarity, coherence, and engagement only.  
- Document changes transparently for version control.

**Inputs**  
- Standardized Thematic Citation Breakdown (themes, citations, grouping logic, polished interpretation).  
- Any base/official snippet or additional interpretations.  
- Primary line text (Wilhelm/Baynes as anchor) and alternatives (e.g., Karcher).  
- Existing cross-cutting notions for the hexagram or line.

**Step-by-Step Procedure**

1. **Review & Compare**  
   Identify strong alignments and minor divergences across all provided texts for the line.

2. **Validate Themes**  
   Ensure every element traces directly back to the source wording and supports the spirit of gradual, patient development. Soften or remove anything that drifts (e.g., overly ambitious phrasing not present in the sources).

3. **Synthesize**  
   Weave the validated themes into one flowing paragraph, anchoring with the wild-goose image or primary line text. Integrate key recurring ideas such as solitary beginnings, hesitant steps, surrounding or past danger, the protective/constructive role of talk/criticism, reassurance of no blame, and the opportunity for patience and clearer understanding.

4. **Polish**  
   Refine for rhythm, conciseness, and contemplative feel. Aim for 120–180 words in a core essence paragraph.

5. **Document**  
   Record version, date, sources integrated, and specific changes made.

• Strengthened the reflective benefit while grounding it in the sources’ emphasis on patience.
