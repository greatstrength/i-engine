# Hexagram 11 - Peace (T'ai)

## Overview

### Summary
In times of peace, Earth above Heaven reflects a harmonious convergence—Heaven rising from below meets Earth descending, blending strength and receptivity at the center to manifest blessings on all. Harmony prevails as lesser conflicts yield to greater principles, uniting Heaven and Earth in balanced order. One fosters this through modest engagement, resolute action, and adaptation to cycles, drawing support from aligned peers. Leadership channels natural gifts for collective good, yet when peace wanes, resistance falters, and local focus prevails. Perseverance amid change secures lasting fortune through skills earned, not force applied.

### Judgement
Peace. The small departs, the great approaches. Good fortune. Success.

In times of peace, lesser conflicts recede, and greater principles take hold, fostering a period of collective harmony. Under the influence of higher ideals, even base tendencies align with a unified purpose, enhancing societal well-being. This alignment ensures good fortune and success for all, as noble governance prevails.

### The Image
Heaven and earth unite: the image of Peace. Thus the ruler divides and completes the course of heaven and earth; he furthers and regulates the gifts of heaven and earth, and so aids the people.

In times of peace, the union of Heaven and Earth reflects a balanced order, guided by a ruler who wisely channels this harmony for the collective good. This leadership organizes natural forces, harnessing their potential to advance human welfare and innovation. Through prudent regulation, the gifts of the environment are aligned with societal needs, fostering prosperity and well-being for all.

- Pervading

### Key Terms
- **Peaceful**:
  - Pervading, great, eminent, abundant, prosperous
  - Fertile
  - Reach everywhere, permeate, diffuse, communicate
  - Smooth, slippery, extreme, extravagant, prodigious

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
When ribbon grass is pulled up, the sod comes with it. Each according to his kind. Undertakings bring good fortune.

In times of peace, one who fosters harmony naturally draws others of similar intent, as ribbon grass uproots with its sod intact. This alignment of like-minded individuals strengthens collective efforts, ensuring that undertakings prosper. Supported by a unified network, such endeavors yield good fortune through shared purpose and mutual support.

### Nine in Second
Bearing with the uncultured in gentleness, fording the river with resolution, not neglecting what is distant, not regarding one's companions: thus one may manage to walk in the middle.

In times of peace, one in a position of strength should approach the unrefined with patience and understanding, while pursuing necessary undertakings with firm resolve, even across challenges. Attention must extend to all, near and far, without favoring personal allies or factions. By maintaining this impartial and balanced approach, one upholds moderation, ensuring stability and harmony prevail.

### Nine in Third
No plain not followed by a slope. No going not followed by a return. He who remains persevering in danger is without blame. Do not complain about this truth; Enjoy the good fortune you still possess.

In times of peace, one must accept life’s cycles—every ascent leads to descent, every advance to return. Persevering through uncertainty, undeterred by subtle gains or direct pressures from kin and peers, keeps one blameless. This truth, unmarred by complaint, reveals fortune not just in present ease but in skills and wisdom forged through hardship, ensuring enduring strength for future challenges.

### Six in Fourth
He flutters down, not boasting of his wealth, together with his neighbor, guileless and sincere.

In times of peace, one of high standing fosters harmony by descending humbly, not flaunting wealth or rank, to join their neighbor. With sincerity and free of guile, they build trust and mutual respect within the community. This modest engagement reflects true leadership, ensuring peaceful coexistence through equality and genuine connection.

### Six in Fifth
The Sovereign I gives his daughter in marriage. This brings blessing and supreme good fortune.

In times of peace, one who aligns inner truth with actions to promote harmony, as if serving Heaven’s cause, receives nature’s support and guidance. Like a sovereign granting his daughter in marriage, this union of purpose and effort yields blessings and supreme good fortune, enriching the world through dedicated leadership.

### Six in Sixth
The wall falls back into the moat. Use no army now. Make your commandments known within your own town. Perseverance brings humiliation.

In times of peace’s end, one faces collapse as the wall returns to the moat. Resistance through force fails; instead, reinforce local ties and clarify directives within your circle. Persisting against this shift brings humiliation, underscoring the need to adapt to change rather than oppose the natural cycle.
