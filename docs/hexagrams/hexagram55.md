# Hexagram 55 - Abundance (Fêng)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram of Abundance, with Thunder over Fire, portrays a time of prosperity brought through movement sparked by inner clarity. As this abundance represents the apex of development, it shall inevitably lead to decline. Therefore, one ought to take advantage of the times and do what must be done while they can.

### Judgement
Abundance has success. The king attains abundance. Be not sad. Be like the sun at midday.

Abundance is prosperity brought about through a great vision and a leader to carry it out. Such a leader must have the inner composure to act and bring heightened clarity and movement to everything like the sun at midday.

### The Image
Both thunder and lightning come: the image of Abundance. Thus the superior man decides lawsuits and carries out punishments.

A leader of enduring fortitude, like Fire beneath the Thunder, enforces the implementation of consequential policies with a clarity that understands the nature of the times and movement that is quick and precise in addressing the problem.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
When a man meets his destined ruler, they can be together ten days, and it is not a mistake. Going meets with recognition.

Should one who embodies the spirit of abundance as does the king step forward to contribute. Although their work may be brief, no harm comes from it. Such an opportunity, when completed well, may bring future opportunities.

### Six in Second
The curtain is of such fullness that the polestars can be seen at noon. Through going one meets with mistrust and hate. If one rouses him through truth, good fortune comes.

One who wishes to enact changes with a superior during times of abundance is impeded by dark forces that exist in between them. Should one seek to engage with the superior directly, the dark forces shall meet them with resistance. Yet should they abide with the times and rely upon the strength of their Inner Truth, the Influence is felt by the superior, ensuring an inevitable positive outcome.

### Nine in Third
The underbrush is of such abundance that the small stars can be seen at noon. He breaks his right arm. No blame.

The situation of abundance is such that even the most lowly are given a platform from which to exert their Influence. This situation removes one’s ability to act as embodied by the breaking of the "right arm," or the ability to stir Movement, but this is unavoidable.

### Nine in Fourth
The curtain is of such fullness that the polestars can be seen at noon. He meets his ruler, who is of like kind. Good fortune.

The eclipse of darkness is on the wane, allowing one and a superior of kindred spirit to reunite once again, leading to positive developments.

### Six in Fifth
Lines are coming, blessing and fame draw near. Good fortune.

A superior is surrounded by capable and trustworthy advisors and works through them to bring abundance to all, leading to positive developments.

### Six in Sixth
His house is in a state of abundance. He screens off his family. He peers through the gate and no longer perceives anyone. For three years he sees nothing. Misfortune.

One wishes to embody abundance but expresses it outwardly using earthly possessions. In their pursuit, they isolate themselves from their family and their community, hindering their ability to act and leading to negative outcomes. They will be unable to do anything for the next while, or "three years," as they work through their inner instability.
