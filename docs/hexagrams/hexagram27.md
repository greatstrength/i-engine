# Hexagram 27 - The Corners of the Mouth (I)

## Overview

### Summary

The Corners of the Mouth emphasizes the importance of mindful nourishment, both for oneself and others, in terms of physical sustenance, social connections, and lifestyle choices. It advises leaders to prioritize nurturing those who can support the community and highlights the need for temperance in speech and consumption to foster personal and communal well-being.

### Judgement

The Corners of the Mouth. Perseverance brings good fortune. Pay heed to the providing of nourishment and to what a man seeks to fill his own mouth with.

This hexagram resembles the picture of an open mouth, serving as a reminder to be mindful of how one nourishes themselves. In the context of one's character, it's essential to be conscious of the food one eats, the company one keeps, and the lifestyle choices one makes. In a communal setting, it represents where the priorities of leadership should lie - nurturing those capable of caring for the rest. It's crucial for one to take care of themselves and what they hold dear.

### The Image

At the foot of the mountain, thunder: the image of Providing Nourishment. Thus the superior man is careful of his words and temperate in eating and drinking.

The imagery of Thunder under the Mountain suggests a scenario where movement or expression is moderated, advising caution in speech and action. This moderation is key for personal growth and development, emphasizing the importance of temperance in one's intake (both literally in terms of food and metaphorically in terms of information or influence) and in how one communicates or acts, ensuring that nourishment, in all its forms, is beneficial rather than harmful.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First

You let your magic tortoise go, and look at me with the corners of your mouth drooping. Misfortune.

This line suggests that one has the means or resources ("magic tortoise") to sustain themselves adequately, yet they choose to pursue what is not rightfully theirs or beyond their current needs. This act of renouncing what one already possesses in search of something else leads to misfortune, symbolized by the drooping mouth, which indicates sadness or dissatisfaction. It's a warning against the folly of abandoning one's secure or blessed position out of greed or dissatisfaction, resulting in loss or regret.

### Six in Second

Turning to the summit for nourishment, deviating from the path to seek nourishment from the hill. Continuing to do this brings misfortune.

In this case, the summit represents a place where resources are scarce or non-existent, while the hill is where sustenance can be found. Choosing to seek nourishment from the summit, a place devoid of resources, out of a misguided quest or perhaps in an attempt to prove one's strength or uniqueness, reflects a weakness of character. This choice to ignore the obvious source of food on the hill for the barren summit indicates a lack of practical judgment or an unwillingness to accept the easier but perhaps less glamorous path of sustenance. Persisting in this approach, driven by pride or delusion, will lead to misfortune, highlighting the folly of seeking nourishment in places where there is none due to an inner flaw or misjudgment.

### Six in Third

Turning away from nourishment. Perseverance brings misfortune. Do not act thus for ten years. Nothing serves to further.

Engaging in the relentless pursuit of fleeting, sensual pleasures rather than true nourishment leads one away from their objectives. This line warns that if one continues to prioritize such indulgences, they'll face misfortune. The mention of "ten years" suggests a long period of recovery or consequence before one can return to productive or meaningful action. It emphasizes the need for a significant time of reflection and change before any further action could be beneficial, highlighting that such behavior not only serves no purpose but actively and severely hinders personal growth and achievement.

### Six in Fourth

Turning to the summit for provision of nourishment brings good fortune. Spying about with sharp eyes like a tiger with insatiable craving. No blame.

Although the summit might lack immediate resources, it offers an exceptional vantage point for strategic planning or foresight. For someone in need of nourishment, ascending to this height to survey the broader landscape, much like a tiger with keen, hungry eyes seeking out its next meal, signifies a proactive and intelligent approach to addressing their needs. This strategic elevation to gain perspective and find opportunities is devoid of blame, as it reflects a resourceful and adaptive mindset in the face of scarcity.

### Six in Fifth

Turning away from the path. To remain persevering brings good fortune. One should not cross the great water.

By deviating from the conventional path, one discovers nourishment from an unconventional or unexpected source, which turns out to be abundant and beneficial. This change of direction, if pursued with perseverance, leads to positive outcomes. However, the advice "not to cross the great water" cautions against making profound commitments or embarking on irreversible life changes based solely on this newfound source of sustenance. It suggests enjoying the benefits while maintaining caution, recognizing that while the nourishment is good, it might not be stable or suitable for long-term, significant decisions.

### Nine in Sixth

The source of nourishment. Awareness of danger brings good fortune. It furthers one to cross the great water.

Having accessed a profound or sage-like source of nourishment, one is now in touch with a wellspring of creativity and potential. However, with this abundance comes the risk of misuse or excess, leading to potential pitfalls. Being mindful of these dangers and managing them wisely will bring good fortune. This line encourages one to make significant life changes ("cross the great water") in alignment with this nourishment, suggesting that the benefits of such a source justify the transformation or commitment, provided one remains vigilant and balanced in its use.
