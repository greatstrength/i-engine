# Hexagram 16 - Enthusiasm (Yü)

## Overview

### Summary
Enthusiasm arises when movement is infused with devotion, as represented by Thunder over Earth, symbolizing dynamic action atop a foundation of loyalty. Success in enthusiasm comes from following the path of least resistance, ensuring that the energy is channeled in ways that resonate with those it touches.

### Judgement
Enthusiasm. It furthers one to install helpers and to set armies marching.

To ignite enthusiasm among followers, a leader must connect with their audience's interests and values. Just as effective laws are those that align with societal norms, inspiring enthusiasm rather than resistance, so too can a leader mobilize support without hidden opposition by understanding and working with the collective will.

### The Image
Thunder comes resounding out of the earth: the image of Enthusiasm. Thus ancient kings made music in order to honor merit, and offered it with splendor to the Supreme Deity, inviting their ancestors to be present.

The initial thunderstorms of summer symbolize this surge of enthusiasm, providing relief from tension and heralding new opportunities. This natural, spontaneous energy is mirrored in the power of music, whether for sacred or secular purposes, capturing the mystery and force that moves hearts and bodies into motion, uniting people in both celebration and fostering communal joy.

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
Enthusiasm that expresses itself brings misfortune.

When enthusiasm is driven solely by personal motives or ego, it leads to misfortune. True enthusiasm must be rooted in something beyond the self; it cannot thrive or sustain itself when it's merely an expression of one's own desires or vanity.

### Six in Second
Firm as a rock. Not a whole day. Perseverance brings good fortune.

A person of superior character remains steadfast and undazzled by the surge of enthusiasm, understanding quickly whether the initial burst of energy will lead to success or misfortune. This discernment, coupled with perseverance, ensures that actions taken are grounded in wisdom rather than fleeting excitement, leading to good fortune through a measured and enduring approach.

### Six in Third
Enthusiasm that looks upward creates remorse. Hesitation brings remorse.

Depending solely on an external figure to fuel one's enthusiasm can lead to regret, as it indicates a lack of internal motivation or inspiration. Conversely, delaying action out of hesitation also results in remorse, missing the opportune moment for action. One must recognize and act upon the right moment, balancing external influence with personal initiative to harness the power of enthusiasm effectively.

### Nine in Fourth
The source of enthusiasm. Doubt not. You gather friends around you as a hair clasp gathers the hair.

An individual with unwavering confidence and genuine sincerity possesses the ability to ignite enthusiasm. This person, free from doubt and hesitation, naturally attracts followers, much like a hair clasp holds hair together, leading to success through the collective energy and commitment they inspire. Their own success and fearless approach serve as the catalyst for widespread enthusiasm.

### Six in Fifth
Persistently ill, and still does not die.

When enthusiasm is obstructed and under constant pressure, this situation, while challenging, serves as an advantage. It prevents the squandering of energy on enthusiasm that lacks substance or direction. This pressure tests and refines one's passion, ensuring that only the most resilient and meaningful forms of enthusiasm survive, leading to a more focused and sustainable effort.

### Six in Sixth
Deluded enthusiasm. But after completion one changes, there is no blame.

Being misled by one's own enthusiasm can lead to poor decisions or actions. However, if one allows this misdirection to naturally conclude and then makes a genuine effort to learn and adapt when new circumstances arise, they can find redemption. This timely, sober awakening offers an opportunity for growth and freedom from past errors, ensuring that no lasting blame is attached, as one has transformed through experience.
