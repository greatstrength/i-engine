# Hexagram 14 - Possession in Great Measure (Ta Yu)

## Overview

### Judgement
Possession in Great Measure. Supreme success.

In times of great possession, one wields abundance born of virtue, ripe for noble acts. Sharing this wealth in deed or spirit reaps supreme success. Prosperity flows from righteous store.

### The Image
Fire in heaven above: the image of Possession in Great Measure. Thus the superior man curbs evil and furthers good. And thereby obeys the benevolent will of heaven.

In times of great possession, Fire atop Heaven blazes clear, illuminating all below with abundant might. One ought to curb base flaws and foster virtue, aligning influence with heaven’s kind will. This shapes wealth for universal good.

### Secondary Names
- Great Possessions

### Key Terms
- **Strong**:
  - Great, big, noble, important
  - Able to protect others
  - Orient your will towards a self-imposed goal
  - The ability to lead or guide your life
  - Yang energy
- **Possession**:
  - There is
  - To be, to exist
  - Have, own, possess
  - Goods
  - Dispose of
  - Arise, occur, events

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
No relationship with what is harmful; there is no blame in this. If one remains conscious of difficulty, one remains without blame.

In times of great possession, one starts free of harm’s ties, blameless at the outset. Heeding the trials ahead curbs pride, keeping fault at bay. Vigilance guards early gains with care.

### Nine in Second
A big wagon for loading. One may undertake something. No blame.

In times of great possession, one commands a big wagon, laden for bold ventures. With means aligned, endeavors rise without blame. Abundance fuels worthy acts, serving all with faultless aim.

### Nine in Third
A prince offers it to the Son of Heaven. A petty man cannot do this.

In times of great possession, one, as Son of Heaven, receives the prince’s vast offering, a wealth bestowed for divine good. Unlike a petty man who hoards, virtue guides its selfless use, free of grasp. This noble stewardship, beyond base greed, aligns abundance with heaven’s will, ensuring prosperity serves all, not one.

### Nine in Fourth
He makes a difference between himself and his neighbor. No blame.

In times of great possession, one mingles with peers of power, where talent and alliance beckon. Yet, holding apart from neighbors, resisting entanglement, bears no blame. This wise restraint guards integrity and purpose amid abundance’s pull. Standing distinct ensures faultless command of one’s own course.

### Six in Fifth
He whose truth is accessible, yet dignified, has good fortune.

The populace is swayed by the genuine humility of a leader of great stature. However, to maintain a dignified governance, such a leader must also be vigilant in addressing any insolence that arises. This balance of accessibility with authority ensures that the leader's influence is both well-received and respected, leading to good fortune by fostering a society that appreciates and responds to leadership that is both sincere and commanding.

### Nine in Sixth
He is blessed by heaven. Good fortune. Nothing that does not further.

One who dedicates the fullness of their possessions and talents to their calling in service to the world finds themselves blessed by heaven. This alignment with a higher purpose clears the path ahead, ensuring that every action taken is beneficial. In such a state, there is nothing that does not contribute to furthering one's mission, highlighting the ultimate success and fulfillment that comes from living in accordance with one's true vocation for the greater good.
