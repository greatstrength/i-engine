# Hexagram 12 - Standstill (P'i)

## Overview

### Summary
In times of standstill, Heaven above Earth marks a rift—strength ascends while receptivity sinks, mirroring late Summer’s fade into Autumn’s decay. Lesser forces obstruct virtue, prompting one’s retreat to inner strength or patient endurance. As stagnation peaks, resolute action restores order, though success unfolds gradually. Perseverance through this decline, whether in withdrawal or effort, secures fortune as base influences wane, turning disunity into renewal.

### Judgement
Standstill. Evil people do not further the perseverance of the superior man. The great departs; the small approaches.

In times of standstill, evil forces disregard the superior man’s perseverance, obstructing progress. As the great recedes and the small advances, one must recognize the peril of this shift. Awareness of these base influences prompts withdrawal to preserve integrity, avoiding entanglement in their rise.

### The Image
Heaven and earth do not unite: the image of Standstill. Thus the superior man falls back upon his inner worth in order to escape the difficulties. He does not permit himself to be honored with revenue.

In times of standstill, Heaven and Earth stand apart, signaling disunity and decline. One ought to withdraw into inner strength, concealing talents from unjust powers. By declining gain or involvement in their schemes, one may preserve their virtue, best managing adversity through quiet retreat.

### Secondary Names
- Stagnation

### Key Terms
- **Stagnant**:
  - Closed, stopped, bar the way
  - Obstacle
  - Unable to advance or succeed
  - Deny, refuse, disapprove
  - Bad, evil, unfortunate, wicked, unhappy

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
When ribbon grass is pulled up, the sod comes with it. Each according to his kind. Perseverance brings good fortune and success.

In times of standstill, one retreats from public view, like ribbon grass uprooted with its sod. Alongside kin and resources of like kind, this withdrawal ensures stability. Perseverance in this seclusion fosters good fortune and success, preserving what matters amid stagnation.

### Six in Second
They bear and endure; this means good fortune for inferior people. The standstill serves to help the great man to attain success.

In times of standstill, one who endures silently aids lesser forces, granting them fortune. By refraining from action, one preserves strength for growth, untainted by unjust powers. This retreat during stagnation lays the groundwork for success when conditions shift, leveraging patience for future gain.

### Six in Third
They bear shame.

In times of standstill, lesser forces rise to prominence, burdened by duties they cannot fulfill. Overwhelmed by their lofty roles, they endure shame, trapped in a position beyond their grasp. This reveals the weight of ill-gotten power, exposing their limits amid stagnation.

### Nine in Fourth
He who acts at the command of the highest remains without blame. Those of like mind partake of the blessing.

In times of standstill, one responds to a higher call, acting to restore order amid decline. Guided by noble intent, one remains blameless in this effort. Allies of shared purpose join in, sharing the blessings of this principled stand against stagnation.

### Nine in Fifth
Standstill is giving way. Good fortune for the great man. What if it should fail, what if it should fail? In this way he ties it to a cluster of mulberry shoots.

In times of standstill’s end, one seizes the chance to restore order, promising good fortune for the upright. Aware of failure’s risk, one anchors efforts on a firm base, like mulberry shoots. This prudent resolve ensures success, turning stagnation into progress with steadfast foundations.

### Nine in Sixth
The standstill comes to an end. First standstill, then good fortune.

In times of standstill’s close, one’s steadfast effort overcomes base forces, ending stagnation. Yet remnants of decline linger, delaying relief with initial standstill. Through sustained resolve, good fortune emerges as these influences fade, restoring order gradually.
