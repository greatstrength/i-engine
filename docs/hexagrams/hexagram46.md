# Hexagram 46 - Pushing Upward (Shêng)

## Overview

### Summary
Pushing upwards represents the ascent from lowly obscurity to power and prominence. Yet such pushing upwards does not happen overnight, as tremendous effort is required to see things through.

### Judgement
Pushing Upward has supreme success. One must see the great man. Fear not. Departure towards the south brings good fortune.

Pushing upwards finds success in modesty and adaptability. One need not fear, as success is guaranteed so long as one perseveres in this way.

### The Image
Within the earth, wood grows: the image of Pushing Upward. Thus the superior man of devoted character heaps up small things In order to achieve something high and great.

One of enduring character, like wood growing in the Earth, gains steady progress without pause.

### Secondary Names
- Ascending

  - Advance through your own efforts
  - Be promoted, rise in office

  - Distil liquor

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
Pushing upward that meets with confidence brings great good fortune.

One begins their ascent into prominence. As the effort has a spiritual affinity with the times, all turns out well.

### Nine in Second
If one is sincere, it furthers one to bring even a small offering. No blame.

Even if a leader of enduring character does not get along well with their followers, should they act in earnest, there shall be no ill repercussions despite the upfront behavior.

### Nine in Third
The king offers him Mount Ch'i. Good fortune. No blame.

A leader has achieved prominence such that they are called to serve as a Divine Medium. This produces great outcomes with no ill repercussions.

### Six in Fourth
One pushes upward into an empty city.

A leader seeks to gain prominence to attract followers, but they search where there are no followers to be found.

### Six in Fifth
Perseverance brings good fortune. One pushes upward by steps.

A leader has risen to prominence through their efforts. Should they persevere in continuing gradually while in the correct path, good outcomes are ensured.

### Six in Sixth
Pushing upward in darkness. It furthers one to be unremittingly persevering.

A leader who pushes through a dangerous and obscure situation must have unceasing fortitude to ensure a safe passage.
