# Hexagram 8 - Holding Together (Pi)

## Overview

### Judgement
Holding Together brings good fortune. Inquire of the oracle once again whether you possess sublimity, constancy, and perseverance; then there is no blame. Those who are uncertain gradually join. Whoever comes too late meets with misfortune.

Just as a Nation comprises many smaller States united under one allegiance, the Waters over the Earth gather, coalesce, and flow together. The structure of this hexagram, with Water above Earth, places a single firm line in the Fifth position, symbolizing leadership. This configuration represents a group of adaptable individuals held together by a leader with unwavering resolve, vision, and experience. It's crucial for this leader to possess resilience and wisdom, leading with genuine purpose to maintain unity; otherwise, the collective effort risks descending into confusion and misfortune.

### The Image
On the earth is water: the image of Holding Together. Thus the Kings of antiquity bestowed the different states as fiefs and cultivated friendly relationships with the feudal lords.

When the cause is compelling but the desire to start anew is absent, one might find it permissible to join an existing group to rise to prominence. When a true rallying point is established, those who join early do so naturally, sharing in the foundational experiences of the group. Those who arrive later are like stragglers, missing the bond of shared beginnings, highlighting the importance of timing and shared experience in forming strong, cohesive groups.

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
Hold to him in truth and loyalty; this is without blame. Truth, like a full earthen bowl: thus in the end good fortune comes from without.

Enduring relationships are grounded in sincerity, much like a full earthen vessel represents a person of great integrity, filled with the essence of truth and loyalty. This integrity, shown through actions and shared experiences, naturally attracts external good fortune, highlighting that a life rich in virtue brings blessings from beyond oneself.

### Six in Second
Hold to him inwardly. Perseverance brings good fortune.

When one is driven to act for those in higher positions, the genuine connection one has with peers is vital. This intrinsic bond fosters a stronger sense of unity than one who is merely perceived as seeking personal advancement. Through this inner commitment and perseverance, good fortune naturally follows, illustrating the power of authentic relationships in achieving success.

### Six in Third
You hold together with the wrong people.

Maintaining superficial ties with those of lesser inclination out of convenience is acceptable, provided one avoids slipping into deeper intimacy through habit. It's crucial to remain open, ready for the right connections to form at the appropriate time, emphasizing the importance of discernment in relationships for true alignment and growth.

### Six in Fourth
Hold to him outwardly also. Perseverance brings good fortune.

One is connected to someone pivotal within a union, offering much potential for gain. However, it's wise to maintain a superficial engagement to avoid drawing the ire of others who might see themselves as more entitled to such a position. Through this balance of outward connection and inner caution, perseverance leads to good fortune, highlighting the strategy of navigating alliances carefully.

### Nine in Fifth
Manifestation of holding together. In the hunt the king uses beaters on three sides only and foregoes the game that runs off. The citizens need no warning. Good fortune.

A leader, firm and unyielding like a mountain, does not chase or coerce but allows others to come or leave of their own free will. This approach mirrors the "kingly" custom of hunting, where game is driven from three sides, giving those with luck a chance to escape, while the remainder are guided "voluntarily" into the traps. This method of leadership fosters a natural cohesion among followers, bringing good fortune without the need for force or warning, emphasizing the virtue of allowing autonomy within unity.

### Six in Sixth
He finds no head for holding together. Misfortune.

One has missed the opportunity at hand, either by waiting too long to join the collective or because there was no appropriate leader around which to rally. This scenario leads to misfortune, underscoring the importance of timing and the presence of a unifying figure in forming or joining a group.
