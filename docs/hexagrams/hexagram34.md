# Hexagram 34 - The Power of the Great (Ta Chuang)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram, with Thunder over Heaven, embodies the vibrant surge of spring’s revitalizing energy, spanning the second month from March to April. This period awakens life from winter’s dormancy, driven by cosmic intention. It signifies a time of transformative power, where alignment with this dynamic force fosters growth and renewal, provided one channels it wisely and avoids selfish misuse.

### Judgement
The Power of the Great. Perseverance furthers.

This hexagram is formed by the trigrams of Thunder over Heaven, illustrating the dynamic movement of cosmic forces. It corresponds to the second month, spanning from March to April, a period known for the revitalizing energies of spring that awaken life from its winter dormancy. This time is marked by a surge of transformative energy, driven by cosmic intention, thus "perseverance furthers" for those who align with this flow.

### The Image
Thunder in heaven above: the image of The Power of the Great. Thus the superior man does not tread upon paths that do not accord with established order.

Individuals who connect with this potent energy are advised to channel it in accordance with its natural purpose. Those of enduring character recognize the importance of not exploiting this energy for personal gain or misdirecting it into endeavors not suited to their path or the cosmic order. Instead, they use it wisely, ensuring their actions resonate with the broader, natural rhythms of life and the universe.

### Secondary Names
- Great Invigorating

### Key Terms
- **Great**:
  - Big, noble, important
  - Able to protect others
  - Orient one's will towards a self-imposed goal
  - The ability to lead your life
  - Yang energy
- **Strength**:
  - Inspire, animate, strengthen
  - Strong, flourishing, robust
  - Mature, in the prime of life
  - Damage, wound, unrestrained use of strength

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Power in the toes. Continuing brings misfortune. This is certainly true.

This line suggests that when one feels the power or energy of the moment at its most nascent stage, symbolized by the "toes," there's a strong warning against persisting with this power for personal gain. The implication is that such misuse or selfish exploitation of this potent energy will lead to dire consequences, emphasizing the need for alignment with the natural or cosmic intent behind this power, rather than personal ambition or greed.

### Nine in Second
Perseverance brings good fortune.

This line emphasizes the rewards of using the great power available during this time with persistence and in alignment with its natural flow or cosmic purpose. By steadfastly adhering to the right use of this energy, one does not seek personal gain but rather acts in harmony with the universe's intentions, leading to favorable results and the enhancement of one's situation.

### Nine in Third
The inferior man works through power. The superior man does not act thus. To continue is dangerous. A goat butts against a hedge and gets its horns entangled.

This line warns against the misuse of great power for aggressive or self-serving actions. The image of a goat butting against a hedge serves as a metaphor for how one might become stuck or ensnared by their own aggressive tactics. The superior person understands that true strength lies not in overpowering others but in using power judiciously and in accordance with natural or moral principles, thereby avoiding the pitfalls that come from aggressive or selfish use of power.

### Nine in Fourth
Perseverance brings good fortune. Remorse disappears. The hedge opens; there is no entanglement. Power depends upon the axel of a big cart.

This line speaks to the benefits of steadfastness when it comes to wielding the cosmic energies at play. When one uses this power with patience and in alignment with the natural order, not only does it lead to successful outcomes, but it also clears the path of any previous hindrances or regrets, symbolized by the hedge opening. The metaphor of the "axel of a big cart" underscores that the true source of this power is one's Inner Truth or integrity. Just as a cart's axel is central to its function, allowing it to move and bear weight, so too does inner truth provide the foundation and direction for the effective use of great power. This suggests that the strength and direction of one's actions are contingent upon the authenticity and moral alignment of their intentions, ensuring that the power they wield is both effective and righteous.

### Six in Fifth
Loses the goat with ease. No remorse.

The "goat" in this context symbolizes an attitude of resistance or aggression, perhaps that which was warned against in earlier lines. With the arrival of peace or a state of harmony, there's no longer a need to hold onto such combative or defensive stances. Letting go of this resistance, or "losing the goat," is done effortlessly and without any feeling of loss or regret. This line reflects a moment where the alignment with cosmic forces or the natural flow of events has brought about a situation where one can release past tensions or conflicts without blame or remorse, signifying a transition to a more peaceful, cooperative state of being.

### Six in Sixth
A goat butts against a hedge. It cannot go forward, it cannot go backward. Nothing serves to further. If one notes the difficulty, this brings good fortune.

This line illustrates a scenario where one is stuck in a deadlock, symbolized by the goat unable to move in either direction due to its own aggressive actions. When one finds themselves in such a predicament, where neither advancing nor retreating seems viable, the key to progress lies in recognizing the challenge. By pausing to assess the situation, one can align with the natural, cosmic energies of the moment, which might offer a resolution not visible through force or persistence alone. This acknowledgment of the difficulty, rather than blind action or stubbornness, allows for a moment of reflection where the universal flow can guide one towards a solution, thus turning potential misfortune into good fortune through adaptation and understanding.
