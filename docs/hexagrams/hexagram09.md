# Hexagram 9 - Taming Power of the Small (Hsiao Ch'u)

## Overview

### Judgement
The Taming Power of the Small has success, dense clouds, no rain from our western region.

This hexagram illustrates a scenario where a potent force is restrained by a seemingly weaker one. Despite the promise of a favorable outcome and the potential for success in the long run, the current conditions do not support immediate advancement.

### The Image
The wind drives across heaven: the image of The Taming Power of the Small. Thus the superior man refines the outward aspect of his nature.

When one cannot make significant, lasting changes to their environment, they should retreat inwardly, focusing on self-refinement in a Gentle way, as symbolized by the trigram of Wood. Simultaneously, any outward progress should also be small and gradual, aiming for minor victories that will accumulate to bring a positive impact at a later time. This dual approach underscores the value of patience, subtlety, and the strategic use of small, consistent efforts towards both personal growth and external influence.

### Secondary Names
- Small Accumulating

### Key Terms
- **Small**:
  - Little, common, unimportant
  - Adapt to what crosses one's path
  - Take in, make smaller
  - Dwindle, lessen, little, slim, slight
  - Yin energy
- **Accumulate**:
  - Gather, collect, take in, hoard, retain
  - Control, restrain
  - Take care of, support, tolerate
  - Tame, train or pasture animals, raise, bring up, domesticate
  - Be tamed or controlled by something

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Return to the way. How could there be blame in this? Good fortune.

One does not find success by stubbornly persisting on a path not suited to them. By returning to the correct path, they maintain their prospects for success. How could this choice be faulted? This line emphasizes the wisdom of recognizing when to retreat or realign with one's true course, ensuring that efforts are not wasted but directed towards a more fruitful outcome.

### Nine in Second
He allows himself to be drawn into returning. Good fortune.

One sees those around struggle and fall before they too could start. By learning from their peers, one has achieved good fortune by excusing themselves early and not kindizing their position.

### Nine in Third
The spokes burst out of the wagon wheels. Man and wife roll their eyes.

One seeks to push forward, envisioning an easy win. However, in this scenario, fortune aligns with the weaker force, leading to a rebuff of any aggressive advance. The rolling of eyes by man and wife symbolizes the disdain or disappointment felt towards the leader's choice, resulting in a loss of dignity for them and a general erosion of trust among all involved, highlighting the folly of underestimating the power of the small in moments of restraint.

### Six in Fourth
If you are sincere, blood vanishes and fear gives way. No blame.

When one must act as the weak force restraining the strong in the pursuit of righteousness, there's a risk of igniting dangerous tensions. However, sincerity in such endeavors ensures that conflict dissipates, and fear subsides. The truth ultimately prevails, leading to a resolution where no blame is cast, illustrating that integrity can transform potentially harmful situations into ones of peace and understanding.

### Nine in Fifth
If you are sincere and loyally attached, you are rich in your neighbor.

Pleasure, when shared, is doubled. When loyalty exists between two, they enhance each other's lives. Therefore, if one feels constrained, it's wise to recognize that such restriction might stem from a place of devotion to their well-being, suggesting that true richness lies in the bonds of mutual support and understanding.

### Nine in Sixth
The rain comes, there is rest. This is due to the lasting effect of character. Perseverance brings the woman into danger. The moon is nearly full. If the superior man persists, misfortune comes.

Success has been achieved through patient perseverance in character and influence, not through force. Yet, boasting of this victory not only lacks merit but also invites danger. One should focus on consolidating what has been achieved, recognizing that to advance further prematurely could lead to misfortune. Like the moon nearing its fullness, the time calls for completion and caution rather than further action.
