# Hexagram 50 - The Cauldron (Ting)

## Overview

### Summary
In times of the Cauldron, Fire over Wood transforms raw potential into refined sustenance, a human vessel for ritual and communal nourishment. One cultivates supreme good fortune through skilled care, purging stagnation or upholding ornate strength. From overturned legs to jade rings, each step—whether clearing flaws, bearing insight, or aligning with purpose—enhances welfare. Perseverance turns resources into prosperity, reflecting inner truth through service to family and beyond.

### Judgement
The Cauldron. Supreme good fortune. Success.

In times of the Cauldron, one fosters supreme good fortune through skilled nourishment of community and self. Unlike the Well’s universal sustenance, this vessel symbolizes distinct groups—families or states—refining resources into prosperity. Success hinges on maintaining systems and conduct, ensuring effective care that sustains all involved.

### The Image
Fire over wood: the image of The Cauldron. Thus the superior man consolidates his fate by making his position correct.

In times of the Cauldron, Fire above Wood ignites transformation, sustained by steady fuel below. One ought to align efforts with purpose, leveraging unique skills to nourish community and self. By securing a rightful stance, one consolidates fate, fostering conditions for enduring success through disciplined contribution.

- **Vessel/Holding**:
  - A cauldron with three feet and two ears, a sacred vessel for cooking offerings, sacrifices, and ritual meals
  - Founding symbol of a family or dynasty
  - Receptacle
  - Hold, contain, and transform, transmute
  - Consecrate, connect with the spirits
  - Found, establish, secure
  - Precious, well-grounded
  - A time to commune with and seek help from the spirits

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
A cauldron with legs upturned. Furthers removal of stagnating stuff. One takes a concubine for the sake of her son. No blame.

In times of the Cauldron, one overturns the vessel to clear stagnant residue, purging harmful influences. This cleansing, open to all, incurs no blame. Accepting short-term burdens, like taking a concubine for her son, serves long-term gain, ensuring renewal without fault.

### Nine in Second
There is food in the cauldron. My comrades are envious, but they cannot harm me. Good fortune.

In times of the Cauldron, one holds valued resources in the vessel, enriched by seasoned insight. This worth stirs envy among peers, yet their unrest poses no threat. Rooted in stability, one’s contribution ensures good fortune, prevailing over fleeting discord.

### Nine in Third
The handle of the cauldron is altered. One is impeded in his way of life. The fat of the pheasant is not eaten. Once rain falls, remorse is spent. Good fortune comes in the end.

In times of the Cauldron, one’s capacity to serve is stalled, the vessel’s handle shifted
