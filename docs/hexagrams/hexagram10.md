# Hexagram 10 - Treading (Lü)

## Overview

### Summary
In times of treading, Heaven above Lake spans father’s dignity over daughter’s joy, guiding conduct from restraint to bold grace. One steps upon the tiger’s tail, navigating hierarchies inherent in nature—simple at first, daring mid-path, resolute in peril, and reflective at end. Hierarchies falter without justice or merit, stirring unrest, yet a society aligned with inner worth finds peace. Caution and awareness turn risk to supreme good fortune, honoring rank through balanced tread.

### Judgement
Treading. Treading upon the tail of the tiger. It does not bite the man.

In times of treading, one steps lightly on the tiger’s tail, a bold yet joyful act unmet by harm. Strength yields to grace, sensing no threat in playful intent. This dance reveals the art of navigating power, honoring place within life’s order.

### The Image
Heaven above, the lake below: the image of Treading. Thus the superior man discriminates between high and low, and thereby fortifies the thinking of the people.

In times of treading, Heaven above Lake reflects strength over joy, a natural order of high and low. One ought to discern rank and merit, adjusting conduct with care. This strengthens the people’s resolve, setting a model of balanced tread.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Simple conduct. Progress without blame.

In times of treading, one moves with simple conduct, unburdened by ties or strife. Staying true to self while mindful of others, progress flows free of blame. This humble step begins the path with ease.

### Nine in Second
Treading is smooth, level course. The perseverance of a dark man brings good fortune.

In times of treading, one moves with simple conduct, unburdened by ties or strife. Staying true to self while mindful of others, progress flows free of blame. This humble step begins the path with ease.

### Six in Third
A one-eyed man is able to see, a lame man is able to tread. He treads on the tail of the tiger. The tiger bites the man. Misfortune. Thus does a warrior act on behalf of his great prince.

In times of treading, one, half-sight or limping, dares the tiger’s tail and meets its bite. Overreach brings misfortune, yet a warrior’s tread for a great prince bears no fault. Duty tempers risk, balancing bold steps with noble cause.

### Nine in Fourth
He treads on the tail of the tiger. Caution and circumspection lead ultimately to good fortune.

In times of treading, one steps on the tiger’s tail with measured care. Skill meets peril, yet caution and watchfulness guide the way. This prudent tread turns risk to good fortune, securing success through vigilant grace.

### Nine in Fifth
Resolute conduct. Perseverance with awareness of danger.

In times of treading, one holds firm with resolute conduct, facing peril head-on. Perseverance, tempered by keen awareness of danger, drives progress. This steadfast tread masters risk through strength and caution.

### Nine in Sixth
Look to your conduct and weigh the favorable signs. When everything is fulfilled, supreme good fortune comes.

In times of treading, one reflects on conduct, gauging the signs of favor. With all steps complete, supreme good fortune dawns. This patient review reaps the harvest of careful tread, crowning effort with reward.
