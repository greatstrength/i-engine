# Hexagram 23 - Splitting Apart (Po)

## Overview

### Summary
This hexagram signifies the transition from October to November, capturing the moment when the last vestiges of light and warmth are about to be overtaken by the encroaching darkness and chill of winter. It reflects a time of disintegration, where natural forces or human actions lead to separation and decline, urging one to focus on preservation and strategic withdrawal rather than new ventures.

### Judgement
Splitting Apart. It does not further to go anywhere.

In these naturally occurring times of darkness, individuals are instinctively driven towards acts of self-preservation, leading to a lack of inherent unity or collective spirit. Thus, it is not advisable to undertake new projects or initiatives, as the natural inclination is towards personal safety and conservation rather than collective action.

### The Image
The mountain rests on the earth: the image of Splitting Apart. Thus those above can ensure their position only by giving generously to those below.

However, this same impulse towards self-preservation can be countered by acts of generosity. As depicted by the Mountain over the Earth, leaders or those in higher positions can maintain or even strengthen their base of support by being open-handed and considerate. By providing for those below during these challenging times, one can ensure a solid foundation of loyalty and support, counteracting the natural tendency towards fragmentation and isolation.

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
The leg of the bed is split. Those who persevere are destroyed. Misfortune.

The forces of destruction or corruption have already infiltrated and begun to undermine the foundations of stability, represented by the "leg of the bed" splitting. Those who attempt to hold on or resist the inevitable decline in this scenario will only bring about their own downfall, for the allies of justice or integrity have been systematically removed through deceit or false accusations. This leaves one in a precarious position where patience and observation serve as the best course of action, waiting to see where the darkness shall advance rather than face inevitable defeat.

### Six in Second
The bed is split at the edge. Those who persevere are destroyed. Misfortune.

The peril of the situation is now evident, with the "bed" showing signs of splitting at its edge, symbolizing the onset of decay or division. Here, those who cling to outdated methods or refuse to adapt to the changing circumstances will find themselves undone. There's no support or aid to be expected from others in this moment of transition or decline, as individuals are more preoccupied with their own survival or interests. Thus, perseverance in the face of such clear signs of disintegration leads only to misfortune, urging one to reconsider their stance or approach.

### Six in Third
He splits with them. No blame.

In this situation, one finds themselves inadvertently part of a deteriorating or corrupt environment, not by their own doing but due to external circumstances. Despite being surrounded by those who are perhaps morally or ethically compromised, this individual maintains a link to a higher moral or spiritual authority within themselves. This divergence from the group's path might lead to isolation or conflict with others, but there's no fault in choosing to separate or dissent from the collective darkness. It reflects a commendable stance of integrity, where the choice to "split" from the corrupt is seen as an act of preserving one's own light or truth.

### Six in Fourth
The bed is split up to the skin. Misfortune.

The misfortune has advanced to a critical point where it now threatens direct harm, symbolized by the bed splitting "up to the skin." This indicates that the disintegration or negative influences have penetrated deeply into one's life or situation. At this stage, the destructive process is beyond reversal or control; it's an acknowledgment that the damage is done and cannot be stopped, signaling a time of inevitable loss or hardship.

### Six in Fifth
A shoal of fishes. Favor comes through the court ladies. Everything acts to further.

In this scenario, those from darker or less enlightened backgrounds, likened to a shoal of fishes, are influenced by the generous nature of a superior authority. This authority garners favor through them, much as court ladies would rally around and seek to honor a princess. Through this act of generosity, the leader transforms potential adversaries or indifferent followers into supportive allies, leading to a situation where all actions contribute to positive outcomes.

### Nine in Sixth
There is a large fruit still uneaten. The superior man receives a carriage. The house of the inferior man is split apart.

The period of disintegration or division concludes here. The "large fruit still uneaten" symbolizes untapped potential or opportunities that remain after the turmoil. For individuals of superior character who navigated the challenging times wisely, withdrawing to preserve their strength and integrity, there's a swift recovery or advancement, represented by receiving a "carriage" — a symbol of mobility, elevation, or new opportunities. Conversely, those of lesser moral standing or the "inferior man" find their foundations collapsing, their own actions leading to their downfall as they metaphorically consume each other in their chaos, signaling the end of their influence or existence in the current cycle.
