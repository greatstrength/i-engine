# Hexagram 1

## The Creative (Ch'ien)

### Summary
The trigram of Heaven doubled symbolizes the eternal strength and vitality of the life-giving Light and the inexorable flow of Time. The Image of Heaven serves as a reminder of the cosmic order present in our lives, highlighting the immense strength needed to uphold it. To embody this strength, one must look to the community, the world, and the cosmos itself for inspiration.

### Judgement
The Creative works sublime success, furthering through perseverance.

This hexagram represents the Divine Masculine, the illuminating Light that fosters life across the globe, embodying both the time and energy essential for fulfilling one's sacred duties. In human form, Heaven is channeled through the Sage, who acts as a Divine Medium, tasked with bringing order to Earth.

### The Image
The movement of heaven is full of power. Thus the superior man makes himself strong and untiring.

Those imbued with The Creative move naturally and effortlessly in alignment with their true nature, navigating life's obstacles with ease. Their presence inherently balances the world around them. However, to truly harness this Creative energy, one must do so with respect and pure intentions, ensuring that any trace of self-importance is shed. This humility fosters perseverance through reinvention, enhancing the natural flow of energy and contributing to the greater harmony of existence.

  - Spirit power, creative energy, forward motion
  - Dynamic, enduring

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Hidden dragon. Do not act.

The Dragon, symbolizing the energy of the Light, lies dormant in its Earthen Vessel, not yet ready to emerge. One ought to be patient; the energies for action aren't ripe. Now is the time for preparation, not advancement, and one ought to resist acting impulsively out of self-importance or fleeting luck.

### Nine in Second
Dragon appearing in the field. It furthers one to see the great man.

The possibility of greatness begins to manifest in one’s field of activity. One ought to consult their Greater Self or another who embodies them as a Sage to plan the next steps.

### Nine in Third
All day long, the superior man is creatively active. At night, his mind is beset with cares. Danger. No blame.

One becomes deeply engaged in their creative work by day, with concerns lingering into the night. One must spend this extra time on their work as much is dependent upon the outcome. However, there's a risk of becoming co-dependent on those served, which could hamper one's advancement. Such is the nature of the times. Balance is essential.

### Nine in Fourth
Wavering flight over depths. No blame.

Like the Spirit of God hovering over the waters in Genesis 1:2, one stands at a crossroads between heroically entering the world or withdrawing into sage-like reflection. The choice depends on the moment's call. Circumstance guides the path.

### Nine in Fifth
Flying dragon in the heavens. It furthers one to see the great man.

Having fully realized one's purpose, their light illuminates the world, influencing all in its reach. Those seeking them are also in pursuit of a higher purpose.

### Nine in Sixth
Arrogant dragon will have cause to repent.

When granted a position of responsibility, abusing one's creative Heavenly power for personal gain, rather than the greater good, leads to self-imposed isolation.
