# Hexagram 51 - The Arousing (Chên)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram, with Thunder doubled, embodies the Eldest Son, the keeper of Movement, where a firm line sparks beneath yielding lines, igniting shock and terror. This sudden upheaval, like a thunderstorm’s jolt, stirs the world from complacency, prompting reflection and resilience. It calls for enduring character to face these tremors with resolve, turning moments of fear into opportunities for inner order and growth.

### Judgement
Shock brings success. Shock comes - oh, oh! Laughing words - ha, ha! The shock terrifies for a hundred miles, and he does not let fall the sacrificial spoon and chalice.

Moments of terror bring to mind the awesome and fearsome power of nature. They come bringing shock and terror, then pass along leaving everyone to sigh in relief. This terror extends and reaches far and wide across the land, shocking all that come across it. Yet despite the terror, nobody loses their inner resolve.

### The Image
Thunder repeated: the image of Shock. Thus in fear and trembling the superior man sets his life in order and examines himself.

One of enduring character understands that their fate shall be filled with terrors both known and unknown. Thus they work to tie loose ends and set their affairs in order before setting off on any undertakings.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Shock comes - oh, oh! Then follow laughing words - ha, ha! Good fortune.

The initial fear of shock comes when one feels at a disadvantage with their surroundings. This fear is only temporary, as relief shall come and all serve to benefit.

### Six in Second
Shock comes bringing danger. A hundred thousand times you lose your treasure and must climb the nine hills. Do not go in pursuit of them. In seven days you will get them back again.

In times of shock, one suffers loss of possessions due to the nature of the times. As this shock is natural, one ought not seek out what they lost but wait for the conditions to stabilize. Once stable, the possessions shall return on their own accord.

### Six in Third
Shock comes and makes one distraught. If shock spurs to action one remains free of misfortune.

The shock of fate grinds one’s movement to a sudden halt. Yet should they use such shock to spur movement from within, they gain a stronger inner fortitude while protecting themselves from future harm.

### Nine in Fourth
Shock is mired.

The terror from the shock is so great that it cripples movement entirely.

### Six in Fifth
Shock goes hither and thither, danger. However, nothing at all is lost. Yet there are things to be done.

One finds themselves at the mercy of shock and terror from all sides while undertaking something. Despite the terror, their undertaking is sanctioned by fate, thus the shocks cannot prevent indefinitely one from completing their goal.

### Six in Sixth
Shock brings ruin and terrified gazing around. Going ahead brings misfortune. If it has not yet touched one’s own body but has reached one’s neighbor first, there is no blame. One’s comrades have something to talk about.

One becomes affected by terror running rampant within their environment. Remaining only serves to harm the situation. Should one recognize this and proactively leave the situation, they create no ill repercussions for themselves despite the rumors spread about them.
