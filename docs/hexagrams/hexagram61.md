# Hexagram 61 - Inner Truth (Chung Fu)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram of Inner Truth, with Wind over Lake, represents the singular point of entry for the life-giving light of Heaven to shine through to Influence the world. It brings to mind the image of a mother hen sitting atop an egg. Within the great emptiness of the egg, a small life stirs. Yet such life could not exist without the life-giving warmth of the mother atop.

### Judgement
Inner Truth. Pigs and fishes. Good fortune. It furthers one to cross the great water. Perseverance furthers.

One with a pure Inner Truth shines forth an Influence that makes even the most intractable people follow. This furthers to benefit. One ought best be prepared to involve themselves in fundamental changes and to stick by them.

### The Image
Wind over the lake: the image of Inner Truth. Thus the superior man discusses criminal cases in order to delay executions.

The wind is invisible as it blows over the surface of the water, yet its penetrating power stirs the surface into small waves and ripples. So too does one of strong inner fortitude penetrate into the heart of the situation from all possible angles to ensure a just outcome. The mention of "criminal cases" and "delaying executions" speaks to a very serious situation in which one must exercise prudent judgement.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Being prepared brings good fortune. If there are secret designs, it is disquieting.

One’s Inner Truth may bear a strong Influence if it is rooted in stability. Should one harbor secret designs, such hidden motives come into conflict with the Inner Truth, serving as a distraction. This is indeed disquieting.

### Nine in Second
A crane calling in the shade. Its young answers it. I have a good goblet. I will share it with you.

One shall always be connected to those that rely upon them in spirit. Not only shall their followers recognize the expression of their inner truth, but they shall also join them in merriment.

### Six in Third
He finds a comrade. Now he beats the drum, now he stops. Now he sobs, now he sings.

When one finds a companion whose expression of Inner Truth aligns closely with theirs, they form a bond in which the actions of one shall impact the other. Such is the case in both healthy and co-dependent relationships.

### Six in Fourth
The moon nearly at the full. The team horse goes astray. No blame.

One develops the strength of their Inner Truth through the help of the master. In their process of growth, the expression of their Inner Truth begins to fall out of alignment with their surroundings. This is a natural consequence.

### Nine in Fifth
He possesses truth, which links together. No blame.

One with a strong Inner Truth radiates an influence that naturally brings people together. There is no harm in this. Likewise, a unity that is not brought together by such an Inner Truth is one based upon deception.

### Nine in Sixth
Cockcrow penetrating to heaven. Perseverance brings misfortune.

One may still speak on the subject of Inner Truth without being fully able to embody it. The reference to a rooster is used here, as only its cry can travel through the sky. Trying to make one’s way like this leads to trouble.
