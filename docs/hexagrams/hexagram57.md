# Hexagram 57 - The Gentle (Sun)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram, with Wind doubled, represents the Eldest Daughter, embodying a gentle, yet penetrating energy that ensures positive developments through gradual and ceaseless effort. In nature, wind breaks up clouds in the sky, making it clear. In a person, it is the penetrating clarity of judgement that gradually brings entrenched negative forces out into the open.

### Judgement
The Gentle. Success through what is small. It furthers one to have somewhere to go. It furthers one to see the great man.

Success is best achieved not through quick use of force but instead through the accumulation of gradually penetrating effects. Effects brought about by force do not have the same lasting duration on one’s character as those that are gradually imparted over time. Thus it is good for one to have a purpose and to seek expert help when looking to grow something with gradual effort.

### The Image
Winds following one upon the other: the image of The Gentle Penetrating. Thus the superior man spreads his commands abroad and carries out his undertakings.

As the winds blow ceaselessly across the Earth, so too does one of inner fortitude shower their Influence over their world through small acts and gradual effects. In this way, the understanding between one and their followers endures, making them capable for carrying out great deeds.

### Secondary Names
- The Penetrating
- Wind
- Wood

### Key Terms
- **Gently Penetrating**:
  - Penetrate, enter into, put into
  - Supple, mild, docile, submissive
  - Submit freely, be shaped by
  - Support foundation, base
  - Wind, weather, fashion
  - Wood, trees, plants with growing roots and branches
  - Things on a base that subtly penetrates them

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
In advancing and in retreating, the perseverance of the warrior furthers.

As the wind blows, one finds themselves indecisive due to an inner instability. This can be overcome by choosing best what is demanded of the times and acting resolutely.

### Nine in Second
Penetration under the bed. Priests and magicians are used in great number. Good fortune. No blame.

As the wind blows, one finds that the nature of problems revealed extends further into the past than previously thought. In this way, one may find great use of magicians and priests to identify such problems of spirit and to remove them. This leads to positive developments and no ill repercussions.

### Nine in Third
Repeated penetration. Humiliation.

One finds themselves in a paralyzing loop of analysis. Having already come to a solution to their concern, they continue to ponder on this issue. This furthers to damage their reputation, as their lack of a beginning speaks to an inner instability.

### Six in Fourth
Remorse vanishes. During the hunt, three kinds of game are caught.

The problems of the past are cleared away when one is able to integrate the wisdom of their experiences into action such that the mind, body, and spirit, or the "three kinds of game," are nourished.

### Nine in Fifth
Perseverance brings good fortune. Remorse vanishes. Nothing that does not further. No beginning, but an end. Before change, three days. After change, three days. Good fortune.

As the wind blows, one always looks over their works to promote that which furthers and to correct the mistakes of the past. The work is unceasing, as any new development shall always merit some form of solution. In this way, one ought to deliberate carefully before implementing changes and watch over the outcome after implementing it. This leads to positive developments overall.

### Nine in Sixth
Penetration under the bed. He loses his property and his ax. Perseverance brings misfortune.

As the wind blows, one comes to the root of their problem. Yet despite their discovery, they have neither the means nor the support to address it. Stubbornly moving forward in this way brings negative developments.
