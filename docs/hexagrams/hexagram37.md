# Hexagram 37 - The Family (Chia Jên)

## Overview

### Summary
The Family, or Chia Jên, rises as wind stirring fire’s glow—a hearth where roles weave tight and influence stretches outward. It speaks to strength born of care, where one’s quiet endurance, like the woman’s steady hand, binds kin close and roots them deep. Each place—leader, nurturer, child—fans a shared flame, mirroring nature’s rhythm of growth and trust. Here, leadership flows not from force but from integrity, words with substance, and deeds that last, rippling beyond walls to touch the wider weave.

In daily life, one might tend this circle with patience, setting firm lines or nurturing bonds, knowing small acts build a legacy. Yet its power lies in balance: too harsh a grip frays ties, too loose a hold dims respect. When held true, the family stands as a living root—steadying all within and preparing them for change, like wind poised to carry fire’s spark anew. At its heart, it calls one to dwell fully, crafting a home where good fortune blooms through time and quiet devotion.

### Judgement
The Family. The perseverance of the woman furthers.

The family stands as a core thread, weaving ties through natural care—a small mirror of the wider world’s ways. One may see in it a steady pulse, where the woman’s quiet endurance holds sway. While some bring strength and hands to build, one might find her persistence threading the heartstrings tight—keeping kin close and reaching out to stitch bonds with neighboring clans. In this gentle, lasting effort, the family’s warmth ripples outward, rooting all in something firm.

### The Image
Wind comes forth from fire: the image of The Family. Thus the superior man has substance in his words and duration in his way of life.

As wind lifts fire’s breath outward, one’s inner truth may ripple through what they say and do, touching kin and stretching into the wider circle. One might find strength in words that ring true and a life shaped to last—each thread weaving harmony among those close. Within the family’s frame, every role fans this glow, steadying growth that echoes beyond the walls. Here, what’s held dear at home mirrors the broader weave, hinting at a quiet duty to carry it further.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Firm seclusion within the family. Remorse disappears.

In the here and now, one may set a steady line within the family—a wall of clear ways to shield what matters. By holding firm from the start, one might keep regret at bay, sparing kin from stumbles that echo outward. This quiet stand, rooted in the home’s heart, draws a space where roles settle and trust holds. One could see it as a choice to shape the present, steering clear of tangles before they knot. With this, harmony takes root, and the shadow of shame fades.

### Six in Second
She should not follow her whims. She must attend within to the food. Perseverance brings good fortune.

Within the family’s fold, one may turn to what sustains—tending the “food” that keeps all whole. Passing fancies might call, yet one could let them drift, choosing instead to stoke the hearth with steady hands. Where whims might unravel the ties, this quiet care holds them firm, weaving a strength that grows unseen. One might find in this a rhythm that steadies all, a nurturing thread that, through time, spins good fortune for every corner of the home.

### Nine in Third
When tempers flare up in the family, too great severity brings remorse. Good fortune nonetheless. When woman and child dally and laugh, it leads in the end to humiliation.

When sparks fly among kin, one may face a sharp choice—lean too hard into strictness, and a sting of regret might follow, fraying ties in the heat. Yet, if guided by care to steady the storm, one could find a firmer ground takes shape, yielding good in time. On the flip, if one lets laughter and light play linger too long—like a child’s giggle or a mother’s jest—where weight is due, a shadow may creep in. Among the close circle, this drift might dim respect, tipping toward shame.

### Six in Fourth
She is the treasure of the house. Great good fortune.

Within the home’s circle, one may rise as its steady core—nurturing life with a warm, constant hand. Like a treasure tucked deep, this quiet care holds the family’s pulse, a gift that hums through every bond. One might pour into this role, tending ties that stretch and strengthen over time. From such roots, a rich tide flows—lifting all who share the space into a thriving whole. Here, good fortune spills wide, carried by the strength of a heart given fully.

### Nine in Fifth
As a king he approaches his family. Fear not. Good fortune.

One may stand as a king within the home—guiding with a steady heart, rich in care and fair in hand. With love as the crown and respect as the scepter, one might shape a space where shadows of doubt fade. No need for trembling here; trust grows thick, a sturdy roof over all. Through this clear lead, one could lift the family toward a shared good—a harmony that hums with quiet strength. In such a light, fortune finds its way, rooted in a higher aim held true.

### Nine in Sixth
His work commands respect. In the end good fortune comes.

At the family’s peak, one stands as a leader whose steady work earns reverence from kin and beyond. Through years of care and integrity, he builds a legacy that glows like wind-blown fire, securing the clan’s strength. His quiet example roots deep, and in time, good fortune flows—a harvest of respect reaped from a life well-tended.
