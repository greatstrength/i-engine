# Hexagram 32 - Duration (Hêng)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram, with Thunder over Wind, illustrates the enduring harmony of a balanced relationship, akin to a marriage where the masculine leads with initiative and the feminine supports with gentle motion. This dynamic reflects the essence of Duration, a state of steadfast commitment and self-renewal that sustains through time. It signifies a bond with one’s life purpose, resilient across challenges, fostering growth through consistent, purposeful action.

### Judgement
Duration. Success. No blame. Perseverance furthers. It furthers one to have somewhere to go.

If Enthusiasm demonstrates the nature of courtship, Duration represents the institution of marriage where the male leads with initiative while the female provides gentle, supporting motion from behind, much like thunder and wind in nature creating a harmonious, enduring relationship. This union symbolizes the essence of Duration.

### The Image
Thunder and wind: the image of Duration. Thus the superior man stands firm and does not change his direction.

Duration is not about remaining unchanged; instead, it's about a dynamic, self-renewing process where, on a personal level, it reflects the connection one has with their life's purpose, a bond that persists through lifetimes. Each new day, each breath, offers an opportunity for introspection and starting afresh. By understanding and aligning with the natural, enduring processes of the world, one can embody these principles, highlighting the importance of consistency and repeatability in building one's habits and routines, using them to serve and contribute positively to their environment, reflecting an ever-evolving, yet consistent, commitment to one's path or purpose.

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
Seeking duration too hastily brings misfortune persistently. Nothing that would further.

Achieving true duration or lasting success requires patience and time. Attempting to force or rush the establishment of something enduring will not only fail to produce the desired outcome but will also lead to repeated misfortune. This approach does not advance one's situation; instead, it counteracts the natural process of growth and stability that duration represents, highlighting that haste in matters meant to last is counterproductive.

### Nine in Second
Remorse disappears.

When one is tasked with performing a duty that might seem beneath them or requires only minimal engagement, sticking to the principles of duration—consistency and commitment—ensures that there's no need to worry about the results. By maintaining one's resolve and continuing to act with integrity, regardless of the task's perceived importance or the depth of involvement required, any initial feelings of regret or concern dissipate. This line reassures that adherence to one's purpose or path, even in seemingly minor roles, eliminates remorse by aligning actions with a broader, enduring commitment.

### Nine in Third
He who does not give duration to his character meets with disgrace. Persistent humiliation.

This line warns against shaping one's character based on transient societal opinions or the fleeting judgments of others. Such a person, who lacks the consistency and integrity in their character, will repeatedly face disgrace and humiliation. It emphasizes the importance of having a stable, enduring sense of self that is not swayed by external pressures or the whims of the moment, suggesting that true character is defined by internal principles and values, not by external validation or critique.

### Nine in Fourth
No game in the field.

Mirroring the Buddhist expression that "one cannot cook a meal from grains of sand," so too can one not hope to acquire anything of value from a place where such value cannot intrinsically be found. This line suggests that even with persistence or duration in one's efforts, if the environment or conditions do not support the attainment of what one seeks, success or gain will not be forthcoming. It's a reminder to recognize when one's endeavors are misplaced or when they are in a situation devoid of potential, encouraging a reassessment of where and how one directs their energy for true value or achievement.

### Six in Fifth
Giving duration to one's character through perseverance. This is good fortune for a woman, misfortune for a man.

This line advises that perseverance in cultivating a consistent character yields different outcomes based on traditional gender roles. For a woman, aligning with supportive, receptive qualities fosters good fortune, as it harmonizes with the nurturing aspect of duration. For a man, overly adhering to such qualities may lead to misfortune, as it may conflict with the expected initiative and leadership. This reflects the need to balance one’s role within the dynamic of duration, ensuring alignment with one’s natural strengths and societal expectations.

### Six in Sixth
Restlessness as an enduring condition brings misfortune.

When one's state of being is characterized by persistent restlessness, it undermines the ability to see projects or commitments through to their completion. While this condition might persist for a while, it lacks the stability needed for true duration. Eventually, this restlessness will lead to the collapse of what one has been working on, inviting misfortune due to the inability to sustain or bring endeavors to fruition. This line serves as a warning against allowing a state of constant agitation or lack of focus to become one's defining trait, as it's antithetical to the principles of duration and will ultimately lead to failure or regret.
