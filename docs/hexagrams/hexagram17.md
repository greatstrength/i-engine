# Hexagram 17 - Following (Sui)

## Overview

### Summary

When the Eldest Son defers to the Youngest Daughter, he inspires a joy within her that encourages her to follow him, highlighting the importance for all, especially those called to lead, to understand what it means to be a follower in devotion to a cause. Whether within one's family or a greater enterprise, adapting to the needs of followers joyously encourages their commitment. A following created through cunning or coercion will not endure, for success truly comes to those who are consistent in doing what is right.

The imagery of Following portrays the happiness that emerges when action leads to satisfaction. This moment signifies a time for rest and recuperation, where one can pursue what their heart truly desires, illustrating the natural cycle of action followed by repose, where both the leader and the follower find joy in their roles.

### Judgement

Following has supreme success. Perseverance furthers. No blame.

In order to lead effectively, one must learn how to follow a leader. Regardless of whether the style of leadership followed is one to emulate or avoid, such experiences are fundamental for one to discover their own unique style of leadership and how it may practically impact those that follow them. One ought to persevere as a follower, even if they are required to carry out questionable, if not risky, directives from their leader in this way, and they shall not be blamed in the long run as they were carrying out orders on behalf of a superior force.

### The Image

Thunder in the middle of the lake: the image of Following. Thus the superior man at nightfall goes indoors for rest and recuperation.

One of higher moral character understands that, in addition to being a spiritual being, they too are an emotional and physical being with wants and needs. Thus, when it comes time for rest, they follow the wants and needs of their emotional and physical states through rest and recuperation, such that they may balance out their activities during the day.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First

The standard is changing. Perseverance brings good fortune. To go out of the door in company produces deeds.

As the situation evolves into a new standard or normalcy, there's an opportunity for positive outcomes through advancement. This is a period where moving forward, especially as part of a collective or in the role of a follower, can lead to significant achievements. The emphasis here is on adapting to change with persistence and recognizing the power of collaboration in navigating new circumstances.

### Six in Second

If one clings to the little boy, one loses the strong man.

One cannot effectively associate with both beneficial and detrimental influences. Choosing to align with the less mature or less capable (symbolized by the "little boy") means missing out on the opportunities and support that could come from being with someone of strength and substance (the "strong man"). This line advises discernment in choosing one's companions or the paths one follows, as the company one keeps can significantly influence their potential for growth and success.

### Six in Third

If one clings to the strong man, one loses the little boy. Through following one finds what one seeks. It furthers one to remain persevering.

Choosing to align with someone of strength and merit means letting go of less beneficial relationships or influences (represented by the "little boy"). There might be an initial sense of loss or remorse in parting with familiar but inferior connections. However, this transition leads to inner satisfaction and joy, as one moves closer to their goals through this association. The advice here is to stay committed and persevere in this new path, recognizing that true fulfillment comes from following the right guidance or company.

### Nine in Fourth

Following creates success. Perseverance brings misfortune. To go one's way with sincerity brings clarity. How could there be blame in this?

Gaining followers through a patronizing or condescending attitude will only draw those who are motivated by self-interest. Surrounding oneself with such people inevitably leads to misfortune, as these followers are not committed to the collective good but to their own agendas. However, when one acts with genuine sincerity, letting go of ego, they achieve clarity. This clarity allows them to discern those with malicious intent and to distance themselves from such influences. Acting in accordance with one's true convictions, free from the desire to dominate or belittle, is a path that avoids blame and fosters true success based on mutual respect and shared purpose.

### Nine in Fifth

Sincere in the good. Good fortune.

All beings require an ideal or a standard of perfection to strive towards through gradual efforts. This line emphasizes the importance of authenticity in pursuing what is genuinely good or virtuous. When one's intentions and actions are aligned with true goodness, without pretense or ulterior motives, it naturally leads to good fortune. This pursuit of an ideal, done with sincerity, not only guides personal growth but also fosters an environment where positive outcomes are more likely, highlighting the value of moral integrity in leadership and personal endeavors.

### Six in Sixth

He meets with firm allegiance. And is still further bound. The king introduces him to the Western Mountain.

One becomes deeply committed to a higher purpose, attracting and binding others in this shared vision. This dedication and the impact of their work are acknowledged by influential figures, leading to their contributions being honored or "enshrined." This recognition ushers in a new phase or role for them to undertake, symbolized by the king introducing them to the Western Mountain, suggesting a transition to a place of wisdom, leadership, or a new level of spiritual or societal contribution.
