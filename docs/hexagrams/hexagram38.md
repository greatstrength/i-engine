# Hexagram 38 - Opposition (K'uei)

## Overview

### Summary
In times of opposition, Fire above Lake mirrors divergence, flames rising as waters recede, yet coexisting in nature’s balance. One navigates tension with wisdom, finding good fortune in small matters through patience and discernment. From lost horses to rain-washed rifts, isolation yields to connection—be it a lord in narrow straits, a halted wagon redeemed, or a companion misjudged as a pig clad in filth or a devil-laden wagon, cleansed by time. Perseverance turns discord into modest harmony, preserving integrity amid natural divides.

### Judgement
Opposition. In small matters, good fortune.

In times of opposition, one discerns life’s natural tensions, akin to Fire and Lake apart yet coexisting. Wisdom distinguishes constructive divides from fruitless strife, yielding good fortune in small matters. This preserves integrity while fostering modest harmony.

### The Image
Above, fire; below, the lake: the image of Opposition. Thus amid all fellowship the superior man retains his individuality.

In times of opposition, Fire above Lake flares apart, reflecting divergent aims like sisters wed to separate paths. One ought to preserve individuality amid fellowship, pursuing small gains over grand ventures. This curbs tension, aligning effort with modest success.

### Secondary Names
- Diverging

### Key Terms
- **Oppose**:
  - Separate, diverge, move in different directions
  - Discord, conflict, distant, remote
  - Animosity, anger
  - Astronomical opposition, 180 degrees on a common axis

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Remorse disappears. If you lose your horse, do not run after it; it will come back of its own accord. When you see evil people, guard yourself against mistakes.

In times of opposition, one sheds remorse, letting a lost horse return unaided. Patience prevails over pursuit, trusting natural restoration. Facing evil intent, one guards against missteps, preserving integrity. Caution averts strife, ensuring peace without fault.

### Nine in Second
One meets his lord in a narrow street. No blame.

In times of opposition, one meets a superior in a narrow street, a fated crossing beyond choice. This chance encounter, though constrained, bears no blame. Amid divergence, it yields clarity or accord, fostering small gains without fault.

### Six in Third
One sees the wagon dragged back, the oxen halted, a man's hair and nose cut off. Not a good beginning, but a good end.

In times of opposition, one’s wagon drags back, oxen stalled, marred by shame’s harsh cut. This rough start falters under strain, yet steadfast resolve turns loss to gain. Perseverance redeems a dire beginning, securing a good end.

### Nine in Fourth
Isolated through opposition, one meets a like-minded man with whom he can associate in good faith. Despite the danger, no blame.

In times of opposition, one stands apart, isolated by discord, yet finds a kindred spirit for faithful alliance. Amid danger, this bond holds true, free of blame. Shared resolve turns solitude into strength, yielding modest gain.

### Six in Fifth
Remorse disappears. The companion bites his way through the wrappings. If one goes to him, how could it be a mistake?

In times of opposition, one sheds remorse as a companion bites through wrappings, piercing barriers of discord. This effort signals reunion, met wisely by one’s approach. Seeking this bond mends rifts without fault, restoring harmony in small measure.

### Nine in Sixth
Isolated through opposition, one sees one's companion as a pig covered with dirt, as a wagon full of devils. First one draws a bow against him, then one lays the bow aside. He is not a robber; he will woo at the right time. As one goes, rain falls; then good fortune comes.

In times of opposition, one, isolated, eyes a companion as a pig clad in filth, unclean in deed, or a wagon brimming with devils, steeped in ill will. A bow drawn in distrust marks this rift, yet patience unveils no true foe. Laying arms aside, rain falls as one steps forth, purging false fears for good fortune’s gain.
