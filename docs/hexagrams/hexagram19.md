# Hexagram 19 - Approach (Lin)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram of Approach, with Earth above Lake, signifies the transition from winter’s end to the dawn of spring, a time when light begins to reclaim the Earth. This period brims with optimism and forward momentum, where joyful endurance unites both the high and the low in shared purpose. Progress requires diligent effort, tempered by the awareness that this vibrant energy will shift with the seasons. By fostering unity and embracing opportunity with care, one channels this approach into lasting advancement, aligning with the natural flow of growth and renewal.

### Judgement
Approach has supreme success. Perseverance furthers. When the eighth of the month comes, there will be misfortune.

This hexagram represents the transition from January to February, marking the first month after the Winter Solstice, a time when light begins to reclaim the Earth, heralding the approach of spring. It's a period filled with optimism and advancement, where the joyful endurance of both the high and the low fosters unity. This progress must be pursued with diligence, knowing that the beneficial energy of this approach will wane with the changing seasons.

### The Image
The earth above the lake: the image of Approach. Thus the superior man is inexhaustible in his will to teach, and is without limits in his tolerance and protection of the people.

The imagery of Earth above the Lake signifies a time when someone of wisdom and joy, from a position of elevation, shares their insights with those below. This approach, imbued with purposeful light, has the power to thaw the cold, rigid aspects of winter, bringing warmth and enlightenment to those who are touched by it, symbolizing the transformative potential of leadership that is both inspiring and inclusive.

- Nearing

  - Draw near, behold with care and sympathy
  - Command to come nearer
  - Look down upon sympathetically, confer favor and blessing
  - Inspect
  - Arrive, the point of arrival, make contact
  - Honor or be honored by a visit

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Joint approach. Perseverance brings good fortune.

The positive forces start to dominate in higher echelons, attracting capable individuals towards this upward movement. It's crucial for those who are drawn to this trend to stay committed to righteousness and remain adaptable to their environment. Through such perseverance and flexibility, they will achieve success, emphasizing the synergy of collective action towards improvement and the importance of aligning one's efforts with the prevailing positive momentum.

### Nine in Second
Joint approach. Good fortune. Everything furthers.

When one is prepared to engage in an endeavor with a genuine intent, without seeking personal acclaim or status, they are poised to encounter great fortune and numerous opportunities, especially when summoned by a higher authority or purpose to take action. It's important to recognize and embrace the ephemeral nature of circumstances, understanding that this period of approach brings with it a wave of favorable conditions that one should utilize to further their goals or contribute to a larger cause.

### Six in Third
Comfortable approach. Nothing that would further. If one is induced to grieve over it, one becomes free of blame.

One might allow the ease or comfort of their position of responsibility to lead to complacency in their duties. While this might seem advantageous in the short term, it ultimately does not advance one's goals or the situation at hand. However, if one is moved to regret or grieve over this lapse, acknowledging and correcting their negligence in time, they can avoid blame. This line highlights the importance of vigilance and responsibility, even when conditions are favorable, to prevent the decay of progress or potential.

### Six in Fourth
Complete approach. No blame.

When one can attract and gather individuals of talent from various segments of society, they remain free from fault. This approach is commendable because it promotes capability and merit wherever it is found, regardless of background, illustrating that inclusivity and recognizing talent across diverse groups is not only just but also strategically beneficial, leading to a more comprehensive and effective collective effort.

### Six in Fifth
Wise approach. This is right for a great prince. Good fortune.

For a leader, particularly one of high stature like a great prince, it is essential to select and appoint capable subordinates to their respective roles. This wise approach ensures that important tasks, once delegated, are executed with reliability. It's crucial that the leader balances authority with autonomy, exercising restraint and not being overly controlling, which fosters an environment where subordinates can perform effectively, leading to good fortune for all involved.

### Six in Sixth
Greathearted approach. Good fortune. No blame.

When a Sage, having previously withdrawn from public life, chooses to re-engage with the world, they might take on roles such as an advisor or expert, contributing their wisdom to enterprises in need. Those who benefit from the Sage's involvement are enriched by it, and the Sage is not diminished or blamed for humbly accepting such positions. This line celebrates the return of wisdom to society, highlighting that such a generous and magnanimous approach not only brings good fortune but also stands beyond reproach.
