# Hexagram 41 - Decrease (Sun)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram, with Mountain over Lake, embodies the act of simplifying and refining one’s life, letting go of excess to focus on what truly matters. This deliberate reduction fosters a natural, graceful flow, aligning with inner truth and authenticity. It reflects a time of scarcity or intentional sacrifice, where sincerity and adaptability fortify one’s character, paving the way for meaningful progress through clarity and integrity.

### Judgement
Decrease combined with sincerity brings about supreme good fortune without blame. One may be persevering in this. It furthers one to undertake something. How is this to be carried out? One may use two small bowls for the sacrifice.

To Decrease is to declutter, simplify, and refine, letting go of excess and consolidating what truly matters for a more natural and graceful flow. This act of sacrifice, symbolized by offering two small bowls, represents a willingness to release superficial conveniences for a purer, more authentic existence. During times of scarcity, when resources are limited, one can fortify their inner truth by remaining adaptable and sincere, emphasizing the importance of integrity amidst change.

### The Image
At the foot of the mountain, the lake: the image of Decrease. Thus the superior man controls his anger and restrains his instincts.

The imagery of the Mountain over the Lake suggests the attitude to adopt during Decrease: firm and unyielding like the mountain, holding inner passions in check. This approach favors restraint over succumbing to instincts, channeling mental energy away from physical fixations towards inner growth. Such a decrease in distractions leads to an increase in wisdom and positive outcomes, highlighting that true progress often comes through letting go and focusing on what endures.

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
Going quickly when one's tasks are finished is without blame. But one must reflect on how much one may decrease others.

This line suggests that after completing one's responsibilities, it's beneficial to offer timely assistance to peers or those in need, especially during their moments of difficulty. Such help, given promptly and without hesitation, is not only harmless but also fosters goodwill. However, there's a caution here: one must be mindful of the extent of their aid. Over-helping, or decreasing the other's ability to manage their own tasks, could undermine their independence, leading to humiliation or resentment. This line emphasizes the balance in assistance—supporting others without diminishing their capacity to grow or fulfill their duties, ensuring that help strengthens community rather than creating dependency or animosity.

### Nine in Second
Perseverance furthers. To undertake something brings misfortune. Without decreasing oneself, one is able to bring increase to others.

This line advises that adhering to the principle of decrease, through simplicity and self-sacrifice, is beneficial during this period. However, it warns against using this spirit of decrease as motivation to start new ventures or projects one isn't ready for, as this could lead to misfortune. Instead, by focusing on simplifying one's own life, refining one's character, and embracing a more authentic existence, one naturally accrues virtues that can benefit others. This self-decrease leads to an increase for others, not through direct action or new undertakings, but through the sharing of experiences, wisdom, or by setting an example. It emphasizes a passive form of increase where one's personal growth or reduction in excess directly contributes to the enrichment of those around them.

### Six in Third
When three people journey together, their number decreases by one. When one man journeys alone, he finds a companion.

This line captures the dynamics of decrease within social interactions, particularly emphasizing the ideal of simplicity and balance in relationships. When three journey together, the presence of a third often disrupts the harmony, leading to one naturally departing, as they become an extraneous element, reducing the group to a more natural, balanced pair. Conversely, when one travels alone, the simplicity of this solitude eventually attracts another of like mind, forming a duo. This highlights the power of two as the optimal configuration for a more pure, unencumbered way of living, reflecting the hexagram's theme of decrease. It suggests that in both cases, the process of reduction—whether through the departure of a third or the addition of a companion to a solitary journey—leads to a more focused, harmonious interaction.

### Six in Fourth
If a man decreases his faults, it makes the other hasten to come and rejoice. No blame.

This line describes a scenario where one has become estranged within their community due to past errors or unresolved issues. By recognizing these faults, learning from them, and actively working to diminish or correct them, one opens the door for1064reconciliation. This process of self-improvement and decrease in negativity invites those who were once alienated or intimidated by the situation to return, bringing joy and renewed connection. Acting in this manner, with sincerity and a commitment to purity, carries no blame. It underscores the importance of personal growth and the restoration of good standing within one's community, highlighting how decreasing one's own shortcomings can foster harmonious relationships and communal well-being.

### Six in Fifth
Someone does indeed increase him. Ten pairs of tortoise cannot oppose it. Supreme good fortune.

This line illustrates a scenario where, during an undertaking, the focus is streamlined and its purpose refined for greater purity and effectiveness, leading to benefits for all involved. The "ten pairs of tortoise," symbolizing a strong message of divine providence, suggest that this fortunate decrease was intended, emphasizing the power of simplifying intentions or goals. When the undertaking's scope is reduced to maintain clarity and integrity, those engaged in it can only experience supreme good fortune, as the natural order supports their collective success, ensuring that all participants gain from this harmonious alignment.

### Nine in Sixth
If one is increased without depriving others, there is no blame. Perseverance brings good fortune. It furthers one to undertake something. One obtains servants but no longer has a separate home.

This line captures a scenario where an individual, perhaps a vessel or specialist in spiritual matters, experiences an increase in insight or power due to the decrease of a Sage, symbolized by the sharing or revelation of sage-like wisdom pertinent to their situation or purpose. This wisdom is potent, offering opportunities for personal growth or influence. The advice is to maintain humility, decreasing one's own sense of separateness or entitlement in relation to the task or situation.
