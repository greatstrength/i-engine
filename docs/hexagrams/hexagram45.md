# Hexagram 45 - Gathering Together (Ts'ui)

## Overview

### Summary
The hexagram, with Lake over Earth, embodies the joyful convergence of energies, like a gathering of people unified by a shared purpose. It reflects a time of massing and clustering, where collective strength and harmony arise from mutual commitment. Yet, such gatherings carry the potential for overindulgence or unforeseen challenges, requiring vigilance and leadership to channel the collective energy toward meaningful ends.

### Judgement
Gathering Together. Success. The king approaches his temple. It furthers one to see the great man. This brings success. Perseverance furthers. To bring great offerings creates good fortune. It furthers one to undertake something.

In the same way that one must gather themselves before taking part in a special occasion, as represented in the hexagram as a gathering of people, the king approaches the temple as the head of the ceremony. In this way, working towards greater inclinations, as represented by the great man, the actions resulting from the mobilization of energies brings success.

### The Image
Over the earth, the lake: the image of Gathering Together. Thus the superior man renews his weapons in order to meet the unforeseen.

The trigrams of the Lake atop the Earth point to a gathering filled with great joy and expansion. Yet such unchecked overindulgences can lead to damage if not safeguarded against. Large gatherings of people and energy always present unexpected dangers.

## Changing Lines

### Six in First
If you are sincere, but not to the end, there will sometimes be confusion, sometimes gathering together. If you call out, then after one grasp of the hand you can laugh again. Regret not. Going is without blame.

Should a gathering form without a suitable leader due to a generally wavering spirit, one of enduring character shall step forward to lead them. If sincerity falters, confusion may arise, disrupting the gathering. Yet, by calling out and extending a hand in unity, harmony is restored, and joy returns. One need not harbor regret; moving forward with resolve incurs no blame, as leadership aligns the group toward a shared purpose.

### Six in Second
Letting oneself be drawn brings good fortune and remains blameless. If one is sincere, it furthers one to bring even a small offering.

One ought to naturally follow those with whom they belong and vice versa. Should a leader find such a gathering, they ought to contribute in whatever way they can. Sincere participation, even with modest offerings, fosters good fortune and remains free of fault. This line emphasizes the power of genuine engagement, where even small contributions strengthen the collective bond and advance the shared goal.

### Six in Third
Gathering together amid sighs. Nothing that would further. Going is without blame. Slight humiliation.

A leader who wishes to form a group is dismayed, as the followers have joined another group. Despite the damage to reputation from being seen as an outsider, a leader may join this new gathering by offering their expertise to a member of the gathering's inner circle. Though met with initial sighs and slight humiliation, moving forward incurs no blame, as the leader’s contribution aligns them with the collective, turning isolation into connection.

### Nine in Fourth
Great good fortune. No blame.

A leader who gathers followers to carry out undertakings for a Higher power while seeking no personal gain shall see positive outcomes and no ill repercussions. This selfless dedication to a greater cause ensures great good fortune, as the leader’s actions resonate with integrity and purpose, fostering trust and harmony within the gathering.

### Nine in Fifth
If in gathering together one has position, this brings no blame. If there are some who are not yet sincerely in the work, sublime and enduring perseverance is needed. Then remorse disappears.

Sometimes, followers join a leader to be closer to their influence. Should a leader be mindful of the ill intentions of their followers, they shall not fall into evil, and the behavior shall be fixed with time. Sublime and steadfast perseverance transforms insincere motives, aligning the group toward true unity and dispelling any remorse through patient leadership.

### Six in Sixth
Lamenting and sighing, floods of tears. No blame.

A leader has missed the opportunity to gather together with others. They cry and lament as a result. Yet these cries and laments bring the others to their senses, allowing for the gathering so painfully missed to occur. Though marked by initial sorrow, this emotional outpouring serves as a catalyst for unity, incurring no blame as it paves the way for reconciliation and collective harmony.
