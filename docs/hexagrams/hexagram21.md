# Hexagram 21 - Biting Through (Shih Ho)

## Overview

### Summary
In times of biting through, Thunder below Fire melds force with clarity, shattering obstacles through decisive justice. One confronts wrongdoing with success, from mild stocks to severe cangue, each bite—whether through tender meat or dried grisly—restores order. Penalties, balanced or zealous, demand awareness of risks, yielding fortune or misfortune by deed. Perseverance and precision pierce resistance, upholding communal good with resolute judgment.

### Judgement
Biting Through has success. It is favorable to let justice be administered.

In times of biting through, one achieves success by confronting obstacles with decisive justice. When transgressions disrupt the common good, it is favorable to administer fair judgment, setting clear boundaries. This resolute action restores order, easing communal tension.

### The Image
Thunder and lightning: the image of Biting Through. Thus the kings of former times made firm the laws through clearly defined penalties.

In times of biting through, Thunder below Fire rouses clarity, piercing obstacles with force and light. One ought to enforce justice with precise penalties, ensuring laws are firm and clear. This deters transgression, guiding the people through understanding and consequence.

### Secondary Names
- Gnawing Through

### Key Terms
- **Gnaw**:
  - Bite away, chew, eat
  - Nibble, bite persistently
  - Arrive at, attain
  - Reach the truth by removing what is unessential
  - Divine the truth from what is hidden
- **Bite Through**:
  - Unite, bring together
  - Close the jaw, bite through, crush, chew
  - The sound of voices
  - The action of covering a pot with a lid

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
His feet are fastened in the stocks, so that his toes disappear. No blame.

In times of biting through, one restrains an offender’s feet in stocks, obscuring the toes. This measured penalty, firm yet lenient, deters further wrong without excess. Justice thus applied incurs no blame, guiding correction through balanced restraint.

### Six in Second
Bites through tender meat, so that his nose disappears! No blame.

In times of biting through, one pierces tender meat, nose obscured by fervor. Against clear guilt, this zealous justice exceeds restraint yet upholds order. Despite lacking finesse, the correction stands blameless, achieving its aim amid excess.

### Six in Third
Bites on old dried meat and strikes on something poisonous. Slight humiliation. No blame.

In times of biting through, one chews old dried meat, uncovering poison within. Addressing past neglect brings slight humiliation, exposing hidden flaws. Yet this effort, though fraught, incurs no blame, as rectifying stale wrongs restores order justly.

### Nine in Fourth
Bites on dried grisly meat. Receives metal arrows. It furthers one to be mindful of difficulties and to persevering. Good fortune.

In times of biting through, one tackles dried grisly meat, a tough obstacle met with metal arrows’ resolve. Mindful of challenges, perseverance sharpens effort, yielding good fortune. Strength and focus pierce resistance, ensuring success through steadfast action.

### Six in Fifth
Bites on dried lean meat. Receives yellow gold. Perseveringly aware of danger. No blame.

In times of biting through, one bites dried lean meat, a subtle case yielding yellow gold’s worth. Despite good intent in the wrong, justice demands careful balance. Persevering awareness of risks ensures no blame, as fair judgment preserves order with wisdom.

### Nine in Sixth
His neck is fastened in the wooden cangue, so that his ears disappear. Misfortune.

In times of biting through, one’s neck is locked in the cangue, ears lost to heedless wrong. Stubborn refusal to hear counsel seals misfortune. This rigid defiance reaps its own consequence, barring redemption through unyielding fault.
