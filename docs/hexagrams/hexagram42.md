# Hexagram 42 - Increase (I)

## Overview

### Summary
Opposite to Decrease, Increase comes about during times of Stagnation when the Fourth Line is gently offered to the First Line, stirring the lower trigram to movement. Such is the nature of both oracles and leaders, to be of service to their followers. With Wind over Thunder, this hexagram embodies a surge of energy and vitality, where the interaction of dynamic forces fosters growth and abundance. It calls for leaders to channel this infusion of energy for the collective good, inspiring action and progress through selfless service.

### Judgement
Increase. It furthers one to undertake something. It furthers one to cross the great water.

Increase represents an infusion of time and energy from Higher powers. A leader ought to take advantage of the times to gather followers and undertake something for the good of the world.

### The Image
Wind and thunder: the image of Increase. Thus the superior man: if he sees good, he imitates it; if he has faults, he rids himself of them.

The Thunder and Wind are a union that increase each other over time through their interactions. In the same way, one of enduring character seeks to increase themselves through their interactions with others, embracing positive traits while foregoing negative ones.

### Secondary Names
- Augmenting

### Key Terms
- **Increase**:
  - Augment, advance, add to
  - Benefit, strengthen, support
  - Pour in more, superabundant, overflowing
  - Restorative, fertile
  - Useful, profitable, advantageous
  - A vessel overflowing with abundant material and spiritual benefits

## Changing Lines

### Nine in First
It furthers one to accomplish great deeds. Supreme good fortune. No blame.

A leader is granted the energy to carry out great deeds. Even if they feel themselves not up to the task, they shall be favored with good outcomes should they use this Increase accordingly. There shall be no ill repercussions from this.

### Six in Second
Someone does indeed increase him; ten pairs of tortoise cannot oppose it. Constant perseverance brings good fortune. The king presents him before God. Good fortune.

One has both the capabilities and the sanction from Higher powers to carry out an undertaking. Only great positive outcomes can come from this.

### Six in Third
One is enriched through unfortunate events. No blame, if you are sincere and walk in the middle, and report with a seal to the prince.

Sometimes, a leader finds Increase through misfortunate circumstances that impact someone else. Should they use this increase on behalf of a Higher power, then no ill repercussions shall stem from it.

### Six in Fourth
If you walk in the middle and report to the prince, he will follow. It furthers one to be used in the removal of the capital.

A leader who serves to mediate between Increase from Higher powers to the followers shall gain the trust of very powerful forces should they use their position with no intent for personal gain.

### Nine in Fifth
If in truth you have a kind heart, ask not. Supreme good fortune. Truly, kindness will be recognized as your virtue.

A leader who has a truly kind heart increases others on account of their influence. The strength of their inner truth serves to further those fortunate to know them.

### Nine in Sixth
He brings increase to no one. Indeed, someone even strikes him. He does not keep his heart constantly steady. Misfortune.

A leader with a weak inner truth is always unstable. Instead of using their light to increase others, they keep it to themselves. This breeds enmity and jealousy amongst the followers towards them.
